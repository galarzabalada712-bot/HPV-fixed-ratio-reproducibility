# NHANES source data and conditions of use

This package uses US National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS) NHANES public-use files and the 2019 public-use linked mortality files. It includes public NHANES sequence identifiers (SEQN), derived individual-level records, and variables relating to health and sexual history. These are derived from the public-use source files; the included records are not newly collected identifiable patient records. Their presence does not establish that every possible reuse is permissible.

Users must review and comply with the current NCHS Data User Agreement: https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/policy/data-user-agreement.html. Among its terms, the data are for statistical reporting and analysis, users must not seek to identify respondents, and users must not link the dataset to individually identifiable data. Consult the authoritative NCHS terms for the complete conditions.

The included 12 NHANES files and their recorded SHA-256 checksums are identified in `reference/official_source_list_103.csv`; other listed study source files are linked but not bundled. The original historical selection procedure is not fully recoverable. No open-source software licence is asserted by this file; author-selected licence and third-party terms should be checked independently before reuse.
